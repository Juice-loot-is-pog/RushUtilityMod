FAQ

Which Minecraft version does Rush support?

The current builds target Minecraft Bedrock 26.63 on Windows. Rush is updated when Minecraft updates.

What's the difference between Alpha and Beta?

Alpha is free and covers combat, movement, visuals, HUD and Baritone pathing. Beta comes with a Patreon membership, includes all of Alpha, and adds the extra mods marked Beta on the Mods page.

How do I get my Beta key?

Join a Beta tier on Patreon, then click Get my Beta key and sign in with Patreon. The key is shown once, so save it. If you lose it or move to a new PC, sign in again and choose Make a new key. Beta stays active while your membership is, and comes back with the same key if you rejoin.

How do I open the menu?

Press Insert after injecting. You can change the key in Click GUI settings.

Where are my settings saved?

In %APPDATA%\Rush\Alpha\configs or %APPDATA%\Rush\Beta\configs, so each edition keeps its own profiles.
